# Surface brief: KeyWar web client (home + match)

Scope: the whole client app shell (home/lobby, queue, match, results). Mode: Experience (the player is inside the game); match HUD follows Operate rules (legibility at max tempo beats expression).

Audience/job: desktop players with a physical keyboard who want into a live typing brawl in seconds.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The match is a Busytown cross-section: every player is a building with its facade cut away, keys ride down your building floor by floor and get stamped on the keyboard-shaped workshop floor. Refuses the dark neon note-highway.

OWN-WORLD: Paper-white ground, flat gouache fills (brick red, school-bus yellow, grass green, sky blue, sand), one warm brown outline on every shape, small handwritten labels with hairline pointers naming everything, chunky hand-drawn display caps.

STORY: The player sees the town, reads "your building" vs rivals, understands keys fall onto their keyboard floor, types, and watches rival buildings lose bricks.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: street panorama; left, giant "KEYWAR" lettering and the yellow "¡A PELEAR!" button with nickname field; right, a cutaway building per mode (3 floors / 6 floors) as the mode picker; signpost shows region + ping. Match: your building centered at ~60% width, rivals flanking smaller.

FORM: Busytown cross-section spread, position 1 of the bolder hand; seed key df245be0.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Signature interaction
A correct hit stamps the crate onto the keyboard floor with a squash + a rubber-stamp "¡PUM!" label; damage knocks a brick out of the rival building's wall (it tumbles to the street).

## Unresolved
- Illustrated characters/vehicles (workers, trucks for skills) need commissioned gouache art; the build uses geometric architecture + labels only.
